Our Learning Programs

Playgroup

A gentle bridge from home to school focused on emotional security, sensory play, and joyful music.

  • 🧸 Sensory exploration & soft play

  • 🍼 Gentle toilet training assistance

Pre-Nursery (ShemPrep)

Fostering conversational vocabulary, motor control, peer bonding, and creative confidence.

  • 🗣 Phonics sound awareness
  • 🎨 Finger painting & kinetic clay
  • 🏃 Agility courses & gross motor play
  • 🍎 Healthy snack table manners

Nursery

Structured pre-writing skills, thematic vocabulary immersion, mathematical reasoning, and stage presentations.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about joining the Shemrock Heart family.

Children can join our Playgroup starting from 1.5 years. We offer smooth progressive stages for Pre-Nursery (2.5+ yrs), Nursery (3.5+ yrs), Kindergarten / KG (4.5+ yrs), and Daycare support from 2 to 10 years.

Yes! Our medium of instruction is English. We use phonetic songs, interactive conversations, and story enactments so children naturally grasp spoken English without pressure, while maintaining warm cultural and regional respect.

Our core playschool sessions run from 9:00 AM to 1:30 PM (Monday through Friday). For working parents, our extended daycare facility provides warm care, nutritious meals, and restful naps up to 6:00 PM.

Through the acclaimed ShemEduMAX™ curriculum, our graduates excel in comprehension, phonics, mathematics, and social confidence. Shemrock children enjoy near 100% success in admissions to prestigious formal schools across Guwahati and throughout India.

Safety is our highest priority. Our campus features 100% CCTV surveillance, child-safe rounded furniture, daily sanitization of toys, secure entry check-ins, fully child-sized washrooms, and female support staff trained in pediatric first aid.
